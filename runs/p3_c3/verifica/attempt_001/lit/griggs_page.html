Jerrold R. Griggs

Carolina Distinguished Professor Emeritus
Department of Mathematics,
University of South Carolina.
Ph.D. in Applied Math, M.I.T., 1977.
Research interests include combinatorics, extremal set theory, extremal graph theory, graph coloring, operations research, as well as applications of discrete math to biology, number theory, analysis of algorithms, and communications.


Enjoying the Oregon coast.

    • Links

    • I have served since 1988 as a judge for the annual Mathematical Contest in Modeling, a "contest where teams of undergraduates use mathematical modeling to present their solutions to real world problems." The Contest is sponsored by COMAP. Thousands of teams enter from around the world. Papers by top teams are published, and several teams are selected to present their papers at meetings of professional societies. I wrote one of the two problems used in 2000 (on efficient channel assignments), in 2001 (efficient evacuation of the South Carolina coast ahead of a major hurricane), in 2004 (amusement park fast passes, jointly with my wife), in 2005 (flood planning below Lake Murray Dam), and in 2008 (melting of polar ice). I also solicited a problem in 2003 (gamma knife treatment planning).
