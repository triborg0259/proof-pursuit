4. The key structural lemma for the colored graph
The lemma below gives the dichotomy needed to control the edges between

lies in one of two small exceptional sets, or a fixed endpoint outside these
sets has weight inversely proportional, up to a factor of log d, to the number
of such edges incident to it.
