% ===== CAMPO: 1. Result and scope =====
\textbf{Official request.} \#\# Parte 5 --- \(d+2\) lines in
\(\mathbb{R}^d\)

\textbf{Punteggio:} 8 points · \textbf{Valutazione:} Judged

The case \(N = d+2\) in every dimension, with the same conjectured
optimum: the \(d\) coordinate axes, two of them repeated. Prove that for
every \(d \ge 2\), any \(d+2\) lines in \(\mathbb{R}^d\) satisfy \[
S \;\le\; \left( \binom{d+2}{2} - 2 \right) \frac{\pi}{2}.
\]

\textbf{What we deliver.} No proof. Reformulation in terms of deficits
(\(\sum\delta_{ij}\ge\pi\)) and sanity check of the values.


% ===== CAMPO: 2. Proof =====
The claim is equivalent to: \(d+2\) unit vectors in \(\mathbb R^d\) have
total deficit \(\ge\pi\). If part 3 is obtained by reduction to a chain
(deficit \(\ge\pi/2\)), the natural route is to iterate the reduction
over two independent circuits of the linear dependence (rank \(\le d\)
among \(d+2\) vectors: the space of relations has dimension \(\ge2\)).
Not developed.
